Sentinel

A private Google account integration for Gary, an owner-operated personal assistant.

Configuration is being prepared for private use. This information site does not activate the assistant or connect a Google account.

Purpose

Sentinel is designed to help its owner work with authorized email, calendar events, files, spreadsheets and documents. Each Google account requires its own explicit authorization. Access is limited by the permissions granted and the application's configured controls.

Owner-controlled access

This is not a public service and does not offer public sign-up. Google content does not itself authorize actions. The public information pages contain no Google sign-in, credential-upload form, advertising or analytics scripts.

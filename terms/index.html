Sentinel is a private application operated by Erick Lindberg for his own use. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up. Only Google accounts belonging to the operator are authorized to use it.

Use of Google accounts

Sentinel accesses a Google account only after that account's owner grants permission on Google's consent screen, and only within the permissions granted. It reads email and calendar information to answer the owner's requests, and creates calendar events, tasks and email drafts only when the owner requests them. Email drafts are saved unsent; the owner reviews and sends them.

Handling of Google data is described in the privacy policy, and complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account settings under third-party connections. Revoking access stops further access by Sentinel.

Sentinel Privacy

Privacy policy

Policy version 1. Google connections are not active in the configuration being prepared. Account activation remains conditional on the data-handling controls and explicit authorization described below.

Operator and scope

Sentinel is a private, owner-operated Google integration for the Gary assistant. It is not offered for public registration. This policy covers the integration and these public information pages.

Google information and permissions

After explicit account authorization, the integration can access information permitted by the granted Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets and Docs scopes. Depending on the authorized request, this can include account identity, email content and metadata, calendar events, file metadata and document contents. Some requested permissions permit sending email or creating and changing events, files, spreadsheets or documents. The Google consent screen identifies the permissions requested before consent.

How information is used

Authorized information is used to carry out the owner's requests, identify relevant scheduling conflicts, maintain assistant context and support configured assistance. Source content is treated as untrusted input, not permission to perform an action. Google account authorization does not override the assistant's separate action and approval controls.

Service providers and sharing

Google supplies the account authorization and APIs. The selected assistant provider is OpenAI; the selected memory-extraction provider is MiniMax. Relevant information or derived summaries may be processed by those providers when the corresponding assistant or memory feature is used. Telegram is the messaging interface and may carry messages the owner sends or receives. Assistant records and memory are stored in operator-controlled systems and may be included in retained backups and archives. These public pages are prepared for GitHub Pages hosting; GitHub may process routine request information such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.

The operator must confirm the applicable provider data-use settings and terms before sending Google-derived information to an AI provider. This policy does not represent that such controls have already been qualified.

Google data-use limits

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google-derived information is not to be sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Account activation is conditional on configuring and operating the integration consistently with these restrictions.

Retention and deletion

Assistant conversations, memory, ingested metadata and related records can persist in operator-controlled storage. Backups and read-only archives may retain older copies. There is no promised automatic deletion period. Revoking Google access does not automatically delete previously stored records or backups. The operator reviews deletion requests and the affected active stores and retained copies separately.

Account controls

The account owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account third-party connections. Each connected personal or work account is authorized separately. Work-account access may also be controlled by its Google Workspace administrator. The operator must reconcile local connection state after revocation.

Website and credentials

These information pages contain no credential collection form, advertising, analytics scripts or application cookies. Application credentials and account grants are not part of the public site. No claim is made here of a security certification, independent audit or universal protection from unauthorized access. For hosting information, see the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, connection issues or deletion requests, contact the operator at lindberg.erick720@gmail.com. Material changes to the integration or its data flows require review of this policy before use.
